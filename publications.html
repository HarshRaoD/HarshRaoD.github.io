Autonomous Event Driven Multi-Agent Orchestration for Enterprise AI at Scale

REALM Workshop at EMNLP 2026

Harsh Rao Dhanyamraju, Leonidas Raghav, and Aaron Lee

Read Paper

A study of how enterprise agents coordinate specialist capabilities over continuous streams of business events. Through controlled simulations, the paper compares DAG Plan Execute and ReAct across agent registries of increasing size, and evaluates task management for prioritization, related-event merging, and preemption. The findings characterize trade-offs in orchestration and discovery; they do not establish production readiness.

Shared Organizational Memory for Enterprise Coding Agents: System Design and Deployment Snapshot

arXiv preprint · 2026

Harsh Rao Dhanyamraju and Leonidas Raghav

Read Paper

A system for making lessons from enterprise coding work reusable across agents. The paper describes a lifecycle that captures task-adjacent experience with contributor approval, curates it into question-answer memories, screens for security and privacy risks, and retrieves relevant knowledge for future tasks. It reports an ongoing deployment snapshot, with effects on retrieval and coding performance still under evaluation.

Multi-Dimensional Hierarchical Data Structures for Analysis and Comprehension Using Large Language Models

Granted US Patent · October 13, 2026

US-12762144-B2 · Co-inventor · SAP SE

View Patent

A method for representing complex enterprise data so that large language models can analyze its structure. The approach converts multi-dimensional hierarchical data into a text-based analytics format that preserves relationships across dimensions and sub-hierarchies, then incorporates that representation into an LLM prompt for analysis and comprehension.
